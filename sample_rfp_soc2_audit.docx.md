# Request for Proposal: SOC 2 Type II Audit

## Firm Qualifications
Proposer must be a licensed CPA firm with active AICPA membership and demonstrated experience performing SOC 2 examinations.

## Audit Scope
The audit will cover the following Trust Services Criteria:
- Security
- Availability
- Processing Integrity
- Confidentiality
- Privacy

## Control Testing Requirements

**Security:**
- Logical and physical access controls
- System monitoring and logging
- Change management procedures
- Risk assessment methodology
- Vendor management program
- Incident response capabilities

**Availability:**
- System availability commitments
- Disaster recovery planning
- Business continuity testing
- Capacity management
- Performance monitoring

**Processing Integrity:**
- Transaction processing accuracy
- Error handling and correction
- Data validation controls
- Quality assurance procedures

**Confidentiality:**
- Data classification framework
- Encryption standards (at rest and in transit)
- Data loss prevention measures
- Access segregation and least privilege

**Privacy:**
- Notice and disclosure compliance
- Consent management
- Data minimization practices
- Retention and disposal procedures
- Third-party data sharing controls

## Reporting Requirements
- Type II report covering minimum 6-month period
- Management assertion letter
- Description of system
- Testing procedures and results
- Opinion letter from auditor
- Remediation guidance for identified exceptions

## Timeline
- Fieldwork: 8 weeks
- Reporting: 2 weeks post-fieldwork
- Target issue date: 90 days from engagement commencement

## Questions
1. Describe your experience conducting SOC 2 audits for SaaS companies with over $50M annual revenue.
2. What is your methodology for selecting controls to test?
3. How do you determine sample sizes for control testing?
4. What is your process for communicating exceptions during the audit?
5. Provide references from three similar engagements completed in the past 12 months.
