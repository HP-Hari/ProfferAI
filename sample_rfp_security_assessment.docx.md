# Request for Proposal: Enterprise Security Assessment

## Section 1: Company Overview and Background

**1.1** Please provide a detailed overview of your organization including years in business, number of employees, and primary areas of expertise in cybersecurity services.

**1.2** Describe your experience delivering security assessments for organizations with over 10,000 employees and global operations spanning multiple jurisdictions (EU, US, APAC).

**1.3** List all relevant certifications your organization holds including but not limited to: ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, PCI DSS, FedRAMP, and any industry-specific certifications.

## Section 2: Technical Requirements

**2.1** Describe your methodology for conducting comprehensive vulnerability assessments across cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP), on-premise data centers, and hybrid environments.

**2.2** What is your approach to penetration testing for web applications, mobile applications, and API endpoints? Specify the testing frameworks and tools utilized.

**2.3** Detail your incident response capabilities and how you would integrate with our existing Security Operations Center (SOC) and SIEM platform.

**2.4** Explain your data retention and destruction policies for assessment data, including compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy regulations.

**2.5** Describe your approach to social engineering assessments including phishing simulations, physical security testing, and tailgating scenarios.

## Section 3: Compliance and Regulatory Requirements

**3.1** How would you assess our compliance with PCI DSS v4.0 requirements across our cardholder data environment?

**3.2** Describe your methodology for evaluating compliance with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) and how you map findings to specific framework functions and categories.

**3.3** Explain your approach to assessing compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy).

**3.4** Detail your experience with assessing compliance against the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Security and Privacy Rules.

## Section 4: Deliverables and Reporting

**4.1** Describe the format and structure of your assessment deliverables, including executive summaries, technical findings, risk ratings, and remediation roadmaps.

**4.2** How do you prioritize and severity-rate identified vulnerabilities and risks? Explain your methodology for calculating risk scores.

**4.3** What post-assessment support do you provide, including remediation guidance, retesting, and follow-up assessments?

**4.4** Provide a sample report template or example deliverables from a similar engagement.

## Section 5: Staffing and Qualifications

**5.1** Describe the team structure you would assign to this engagement, including roles, certifications, and years of experience for each team member.

**5.2** Provide detailed biographies for the proposed engagement lead, including relevant experience with organizations of similar size and complexity.

**5.3** How do you ensure continuity of personnel throughout the engagement and what is your process for staffing changes?

## Section 6: Timeline and Pricing

**6.1** Provide a detailed project timeline for a comprehensive security assessment including all phases from scoping through final reporting.

**6.2** Provide a cost breakdown for each phase of the assessment, including any optional services or add-ons.

**6.3** What are your standard commercial terms, including payment schedules, cancellation policies, and intellectual property ownership of assessment findings?

## Section 7: References and Past Performance

**7.1** Provide at least three client references from similar engagements conducted within the past 24 months, including point of contact, engagement scope, and duration.

**7.2** Describe any lessons learned from past engagements and how you have incorporated feedback into your assessment methodology.
